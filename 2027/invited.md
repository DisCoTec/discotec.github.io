Bio
: 

    [Nobuko Yoshida](https://www.cs.ox.ac.uk/people/nobuko.yoshida/) received her BSc and MSc from [Keio University](https://www.keio.ac.jp/en/) (Japan), before receiving [her PhD](https://doi.org/10.11501/3118432) from [Keio University](https://www.keio.ac.jp/en/) (Japan) and [the University of Manchester](https://www.manchester.ac.uk/) (United Kingdom).
    She has worked at the [Imperial College London](https://www.imperial.ac.uk/) (United Kingdom) and the [University of Oxford](https://www.ox.ac.uk/) (United Kingdom), where she currently holds the [Christopher Strachey Chair of Computer Science](https://www.cs.ox.ac.uk/news/2070-full.html).
    She is also an [Honorary Senior Research Fellow](https://www.gla.ac.uk/schools/computing/staff/index.html/staffcontact/person/33dde6ed8793) at the [University of Glasgow](https://www.gla.ac.uk/). 

    Her research is focused on theories and applications of protocols specifications and verifications, mobile concurrent computation and web services.
    Among her many contributions, stand out [multiparty session types](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-030-36987-3_5), which widened the scope of applications of session types and facilitated [implementing them in many different programming languages](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Session_type#Implementations).
    Her contributions spans many area of concurrent computation, from [complexity analysis](https://doi.org/10.1145/3704872) to [formal verification](https://doi.org/10.4230/LIPIcs.ITP.2026.4) of multiparty session types, from [reversible process algebra](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jlamp.2021.100686) to [compositionality of choreographies](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-642-40184-8_30), along with contributions to the [Java](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-540-70592-5_22), [F#](https://doi.org/10.1145/3178372.317949), [go](https://doi.org/10.1145/3428217) and [Rocq](https://doi.org/10.1145/3704872) programming languages, to name a few.

    She is an editor of the [ACM Transactions on Programming Languages and Systems](https://dl.acm.org/journal/toplas/editorial-board), [ACM Formal Aspects of Computing](https://dl.acm.org/journal/fac/editorial-board), [Mathematical Structures in Computer Science](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/mathematical-structures-in-computer-science/information/about-this-journal/editorial-board), [Journal of Logical Algebraic Methods in Programming](https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/journal-of-logical-and-algebraic-methods-in-programming/about/editorial-board), and [the chief editor of the Concurrency Column](https://eatcs.org/index.php/board-of-editors) for the [EATCS Bulletin](http://eatcs.org/index.php/eatcs-bulletin).
    She is regularly invited to serve in the program committees of CONCUR, ESOP, CAV, POPL, to name a few.
    She has been supported by the [EPSRC](https://www.ukri.org/councils/epsrc/) ([Morello-HAT](https://web.archive.org/web/20240806104845/https://gow.epsrc.ukri.org/NGBOViewGrant.aspx?GrantRef=EP/X015955/1), [AppControl](https://web.archive.org/web/20210126101934/https://gtr.ukri.org/projects?ref=EP%2FV000462%2F1), …), received the ["Most Influential POPL Paper Award" in 2018](https://sigplan.org/Awards/POPL/#2018_Kohei_Honda__Nobuko_Yoshida__Marco_Carbone), [CORDINATION Best Paper Award in 2023](https://mrg.cs.ox.ac.uk/news/coordination-2023-best-paper/), and ["10 Year Most Influential Paper Award" from PPDP in 2024](https://ppdp2024.github.io/), among others.
